About me

Design Philosophy

Turning complexity into clarity

The most valuable design work happens at the intersection of user needs and business complexity. At staff level, the job isn't just designing interfaces — it's framing the right problems, aligning cross-functional teams, and ensuring design decisions hold up at scale.

I'm drawn to products that developers and enterprises depend on, where clarity and trust aren't nice-to-haves — they're requirements. That means going deep on research, facilitating alignment before pixels, and measuring what actually changes after shipping.

Specialization

How I work

I lead with research before wireframes. I use workshops to build alignment early — so teams ship with confidence, not hope. And I collaborate closely with engineering and product to navigate real constraints, not work around them.

Outside of work

Married 12 years, three boys. When I'm not designing, you'll find me mountain biking, hiking, building something in the garage, or deep in a game of D&D.

How I got into design

My path started in marketing, where I learned to think about user behavior and conversion — not just aesthetics. I moved into web development to build things myself, which gave me a lasting appreciation for technical constraints and what's actually feasible to ship.

I discovered product design through the gap between "this looks good" and "this actually works." A few years of side projects turned into a full-time career, and I've been designing complex digital products ever since.
